Three kinds of battle NPC live in PokéSurvival. All of them are click-to-battle, all run on a per-trainer cooldown that starts when you **engage** (a loss still spends it), and all are gated on your badges so you are never offered a fight your Pokémon are not allowed to win.

---

## Static trainers

Fixed NPCs placed around the world, in six tiers. The tier label and colour on the nameplate tell you what you are walking into.

| Tier | Team levels | Requires | Cooldown (weekend / normal) | Pokémon fielded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | 6–11 | — | 7m30s / 15m | 2 |
| Easy | 15–26 | Badge 1 | 15m / 30m | 3 |
| Medium | 33–44 | Badge 3 | 22m30s / 45m | 4 |
| Hard | 47–59 | Badge 4 | 1h / 2h | 4 |
| Veteran | 66–74 | Badge 6 | 3h / 6h | 5 |
| Expert | 75–90 | Badge 7 | 5h / 10h | 6 |

Each tier's ceiling sits just under the obedience cap its badge grants (see [Gyms](../gyms/)). Rewards scale with tier: coins always, tokens with a chance at the lower tiers and a better chance plus Battle Points at Veteran and Expert. Weekend events halve the cooldown.

## Roaming trainers

Nine trainers who take turns appearing in the world — roughly one present for about 25 minutes out of each hour. Their team levels **scale to your trainer level**, in bands that track the badge caps:

| Your trainer level | Team levels |
| --- | --- |
| under 10 | 6–11 |
| 10+ | 14–23 |
| 15+ | 23–32 |
| 19+ | 33–42 |
| 27+ | 47–60 |
| 35+ | 67–80 |

Flat 25-minute cooldown per trainer.

## Iconic trainers

Once you hold **all eight badges**, the Iconic rotation opens: 45 well-known trainers from across the regions, each fielding a **level 100** team of six drawn from a larger roster. One is present at a time, appearing every 90 minutes to 2½ hours. Rewards are currency-only — coins, tokens and Battle Points — with a jackpot roll, doubled during double-reward weekends.
