PokéSurvival has three jobs. You sign up with **Employer Michael** at the Employment Office at spawn (see [Spawn](../spawn/)). Each job pays for the ordinary work you would be doing anyway, and each has its own set of **custom finds** that only that job turns up.

---

## Miner

Mining stone and ore can turn up two kinds of extra find:

- **Gemstones** — custom gems that sell at the **Gem Market**.
- **Evolution-stone shards** — collect enough of one kind and craft them into the matching Evolution Stone. This is how evolution stones are obtained in PokéSurvival; they are not sold.

## Excavation

Digging through dirt, sand, gravel and similar blocks can turn up **fossil shards**. Combine a full set into a **fossil**, then take it to **Scientist Petra** at spawn, who revives it into the Pokémon.

## Quick reference

| Job | Custom finds | Turn in at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Miner** | Gemstones; evolution-stone shards | Gem Market; craft shards into stones |
| **Excavation** | Fossil shards | Combine, then Scientist Petra at spawn |
| **Fisherman** | Sellable loot; gear enchants | Fish Market; apply enchants yourself |

Job earnings are part of the seasonal economy and reset with the season — see [Seasons](../seasons/).
