The PokéSurvival **Safari Zone** sits at the north edge of spawn (warp: Safari Zone). It works like the [PokéWorld Safari Zone](/safari-zone/) — pay the **Safari Ranger**, take your Safari Balls, and catch what you can in tall grass and water until the balls run out or you step over the boundary — with three differences:

- It costs **1,000 coins** a run.
- There are **no Pokéblock Feeders**.
- The roster **rotates by calendar quarter**. Each area has four different pools across the year, so a species you want may only appear for three months.

Every Pokémon here is **level 5**. The encounter menu (Throw Ball, Razz Berry, Throw Rock, Run), flee and catch behaviour, and the hidden-ability and shiny-window rolls are the same as the PokéWorld zone — see [that guide](/safari-zone/#the-encounter-menu) for the details.

## Entry & Safari Balls

- **Cost:** 1,000 coins, paid to the Safari Ranger at the gate.
- **Base balls:** 30 per run.
- **Rank bonus** on top: Pro +5, Elite +10, Expert +15, Champion +20, Legend +25.
- **Event bonuses** stack on top of the rank bonus when one is running.

Running out of balls returns you to the gate. Leaving the zone's boundary ends the run early — *"You left the Safari Zone!"* — and any unspent balls are forfeit. Pokémon cannot be summoned inside, and the whole zone grants water breathing so the surfing areas are safe to explore.

## Tips

- **Check the month first.** If what you want is in a different column, it is not in the zone right now — no amount of balls will change that.
- **Starters are seasonal.** The Kanto through Unova starters are scattered across the areas as Ultra-Rares, each in only one or two windows. The Entrance, Orange Desert, Eastern Desert and Jungle areas hold the Grass and Fire starters; the North River holds the Water ones.
- **Lapras has two homes.** The Desert Oasis in Q3–Q4 and the East Pond in Q3–Q4.
- **Save balls for the bottom tier.** The babies of the pseudo-legendary lines — Bagon, Deino, Beldum, Larvitar, Gible, Axew — are the rarest rolls in the zone, each limited to one or two windows.
