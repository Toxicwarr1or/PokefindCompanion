## Leaderboards

Holograms outside the tower track the **top round reached per format**, both all-time and for the current season. Seasonal boards reset with the season.

## Battle Shop

The Battle Shop next to the Registrar spends the Battle Points you earn in the tower (and from Veteran/Expert and Iconic trainers — see [Trainer NPCs](../trainers/)).
