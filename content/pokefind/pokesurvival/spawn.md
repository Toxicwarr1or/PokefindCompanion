Spawn is the one built-up area in PokéSurvival. Everything structured lives here: the Professor, the gyms, the Elite Four, the Battle Tower, the portals and the utility NPCs. The wild starts when you leave through a portal or `/wild` (see [The wild](../wild/)).

`/spawn` returns you here from anywhere. `/poi` opens the points-of-interest menu and sets a map arrow to whichever entry you pick.

Coordinates below are **(x, y, z)** in the spawn world.

---

## Getting started

**Professor Redwood** runs the Lab at the top of the hill. On your first visit he walks you through picking a starter and hands you your first Poké Balls. He also holds the tutorials — come back to him for the Poké Ball crafting guide and others as they are added.

Starters on offer are the fifteen gen 1–5 starters: Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile, Treecko, Torchic, Mudkip, Turtwig, Chimchar, Piplup, Snivy, Tepig, Oshawott.
